# Emvryx: a guide for family and friends

No technical words needed. This explains what the different Emvryx models are, how they differ, and which one to pick.

## What is Emvryx?

Emvryx is an AI assistant you can talk to, like a very well-read helper. Ask it a question, get help with homework, write a letter, plan a trip, or fix a small piece of computer code. It lives on a computer we run, and you reach it from your phone or a web page with your own private key.

It is built to be honest. It looks things up and tells you where the answer came from. If it doesn't know, it says "I don't know" instead of guessing. Like any AI it can still make mistakes, so check anything important: homework, health and money.

## Think of it as a family of helpers

Emvryx comes in five sizes. A bigger one is smarter and more careful, but a little slower. A smaller one is quicker and lighter.

| Name | Think of it as | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | A quick friend | Simple questions, short answers, older phones |
| **Flare** | A bright, speedy helper | Everyday questions and quick drafts |
| **Equinox** | A capable all-rounder | Homework, writing, maths, and small coding jobs |
| **Solstice** | A careful expert | Harder questions, long writing, bigger coding jobs |
| **Apogee** | The top of the line (trained, being tested) | Your toughest problems |

**The simple rule:** start with the smaller one. If the answer isn't good enough, switch to the next one up.

## What will you actually notice?

- **Ember and Flare 2** (our first, home-grown models) are friendly and quick, but they are small. They can mix things up or lose track of a long chat.
- **Flare 2.5** is a big step up. Answers are fuller and it can do basic maths. It is still a "quick answers" model.
- **Equinox** is where it starts to feel like a real study buddy. It is good at maths word problems and explaining things, and it can help with small coding tasks.
- **Solstice** is more careful and thorough. It is the one to pick for a long essay, a tricky question, or a bigger coding job.
- **Apogee** is for the hardest work. It is trained and being tested; it needs a strong computer, so it arrives last.

In our own tests, Equinox solved about three in four maths word problems, Flare 2.5 about two in five, and the very first models almost none. Tests are only a guide; how it feels to use is what matters.

## Why do the names sound like space?

The names come from the sky. An ember is a glowing spark, a flare is a burst of light, the equinox is when day and night balance, the solstice is the sun at its peak, and the apogee is the farthest point of an orbit. They go from small to grand.

## What is "Flare 2" versus "Flare 2.5"?

Version numbers tell you how it was made:
- **The "2" models (Ember 2, Flare 2)** were built completely from scratch by us. Smaller, and ours from the ground up.
- **The "2.5" and bigger models** start from an open, freely shared AI and are then taught our own lessons: look things up, name the source, say "I don't know", and follow your rules. They are much more capable. We credit the open model that they start from.

## New helpers inside Emvryx

- **Skills:** pick a style for a chat: study buddy, teacher, writing coach, storyteller, IT helper, recipe helper or planner.
- **Teacher mode:** makes lesson plans, quizzes, rubrics and parent notes, and does grade maths (class averages, weighted grades, "what do I need on the final"). Save the result as a PDF, Word file or Google Doc.
- **Documents:** upload a PDF, Word or text file, attach it to a chat, and ask questions about it. Answers use your file first.
- **Live facts:** for things like today's date, exchange rates, sports scores, weather history or a quick calculation, it uses a trusted free source and tells you which one. This is skipped in private chats.

## VryxCode: the coding helper

**VryxCode** is a part of Emvryx that helps write computer code. You tell it what you want, and it makes a small project, tries it out, fixes what fails, and shows you every step.

- It asks you before it changes anything, with "Allow" or "No" buttons.
- It only works inside its own project folder, so it can't touch anything else.
- It is only on **Equinox, Solstice and Apogee**, because the smaller models are too small to do coding well. On Equinox, give it small jobs (one function or one file).
- You can download what it made as a zip file, or upload your own project for it to work on.

## How much can I use it? (Usage)

Each plan has a "tank" and a weekly allowance. Your first message starts a 6-hour window; when it ends, the tank is full again (restarting the app does not refill it). Every message uses a little, and bigger models and longer answers use more. Coding with VryxCode uses a lot more than chatting, because it works in many steps. The screen always shows what you have left, and it tells you clearly when you've run out. Nothing is charged by surprise.

## Your privacy

- Your chats are saved on our server, and only you (and the owner) can see them. Private chats are never saved at all.
- Your key is like a house key. Don't share it, and don't post it where others can see it. If you think someone saw it, tell the owner and they will give you a new one.
- Nothing you type is used to train the model without you knowing.

## Quick answers to common questions

**Why did it say "I don't know"?** That is on purpose. A wrong answer said confidently is worse than an honest "I don't know".

**Why did it get something wrong?** All AI does sometimes. Ask it to check, ask it to show the source, or switch to a bigger model.

**Which model should I use for homework?** Equinox for most things. Solstice for an essay or a very hard problem.

**Does my phone need to be fast?** No. The AI runs on our computer, not your phone.

**Is it free?** Plans are Free, Pro and Mega. The bigger models need a paid plan.
