# Ideas to make Emvryx different (to review, Oct 2026)

Agreed to look at these together over the weekend. Nothing here is built yet. Suggested order: 1, 2, 3.

## Trust and honesty (Emvryx's strongest trait)
1. **Receipts on every answer.** Tap a reply to see what it used: the notes it read, the live source, whether it answered from memory or looked it up, and when it is guessing. Builds on lookups and live facts.
2. **"What left this computer" log.** A list of every outside request the app made (weather, search, rates, news), with time and source. Chats and files stay local.
3. **Second opinion.** A "Check this" button asks a different model and shows where the two agree or disagree. Auto mode can start with Flare and move up to a bigger model when unsure. Local models make a second opinion free of per-use fees.
4. **Never guesses a number.** For any numeric answer the model writes a few lines of code, runs them, and shows the working (today only the calculators do this).

## Organising your work
5. **Projects (spaces).** A folder per topic (Homework, My business, Cooking) with its own documents, instructions, memory and chats.

## Family and teacher audience
6. **Family accounts.** A parent dashboard with usage budgets per person, a kid-safe mode with reading level, and a study helper that guides instead of giving the answer.
7. **Learning loop.** Flashcards and quizzes made from your own documents, a "what you got wrong" review, and student progress in Teacher mode.

## VryxCode
8. **Explain mode.** "Explain each step in plain English" and "teach me what you just built", for people who are not programmers.

## Cheap wins
9. **Voice.** Dictation and read-aloud with the browser's built-in speech features.
10. **Public scorecards with a "where it fails" note** for each model, next to the measured scores already on the website.
