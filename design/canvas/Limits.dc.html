Sparks and limits

Only you see this. Limits apply to models served from your PC or a server, never to a model running on a person’s own device. 1 spark = 1,000 counted tokens.
1. The tankshort-term: how much at once
Empty to full in 8 hours. It refills steadily, so there is no “wait for the reset” cliff.
2. The weeklong-term ceiling
The total of every chat in the week. The tank can be full and the week still empty, or the other way round; the smaller one wins.
3. One requestnot an allowance
The context window: the most one message may carry. 32,768 is not “32,000 every few hours”; it only caps a single request.
