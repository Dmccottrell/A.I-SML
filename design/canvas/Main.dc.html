What it does

Private by default

The model is a file on your device. No account, no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you switch on remote mode or web search, and the screen always says when.

Remembers you, on your terms

It can save facts like your name, finds earlier chats by meaning, never saves passwords, and has a Memory screen where you can edit or delete anything. Private chats are never stored.

Looks things up and shows its sources

Wikipedia is built in. Web search is off by default; when it is on, answers name the site and date. If the notes don’t say, it says it doesn’t know.

Skills for real jobs

A Study helper and a Teacher assistant (both Beta) make worksheets, reading passages and lesson plans, and save them as a PDF or Word file.

WHAT YUVRA IS

Built from the ground up.

Most AI apps are a thin layer on someone else’s model. Yuvra is the model: its own tokenizer, its own transformer and its own training run, with no borrowed weights and no other assistant’s answers in its lessons.

Its own brain

We wrote the tokenizer, the transformer and the training loop, then trained it on carefully chosen reading: educational pages, everyday web text, all of English Wikipedia, code in many languages and step-by-step maths.

Taught like a good assistant

It answers from its notes and names the source. It says “I don’t know” instead of guessing, rechecks when you say it’s wrong, keeps to exact counts and formats, follows a change of topic, and reads through typos.

A family that keeps improving

Every new version keeps everything the last one learned, and must beat it on the same tests before it replaces it. Bigger siblings follow, and the app stays the same.

Like any AI, Yuvra can make mistakes. That is why it shows its sources and which model answered, so you can check what matters.

At home or in the cloud. Private or public.

Any Yuvra model can run wherever you want it, and be shared as widely as you want. Same models, same app; only the address and the guest list change.
