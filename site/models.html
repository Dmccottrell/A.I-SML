THE MODELS

Five models. Pick one, or let Auto choose.

Each one has its own colour and its own job. A newer version of a model replaces the older one, and the older one stays available under “Other models”.

EmberAVAILABLE NOW · BETA
The small first assistant: quick, light and happy on almost any device. Smouldering red with a glowing core, the spark before a flare. Ember 2 is in private beta with a small test group.
Plans: Free, Pro, Mega
Effort: Quick, Balanced
Best for: simple questions, quick drafts, older phones
FlareBETA
Fast and light, for quick answers. Hot red-orange: a sudden burst of light. Flare 2.5 is tuned here on Emvryx’s own lessons: 65.3% on the full HellaSwag test, level with the base it started from, and a perfect identity test. In private beta.
Plans: Free, Pro, Mega
Effort: Quick, Balanced, Deep
Best for: short questions, small tasks
EquinoxBETA
For everyday work and homework, with skills and tools. Black and white: balance between light and dark. Equinox 2 is tuned here on Emvryx’s own lessons: 69.1% on the full HellaSwag test (plain base about 65%), a perfect identity test, and 62% on the MBPP coding test. It runs VryxCode, our coding assistant, for small tasks.
Plans: Free, Pro, Mega
Effort: Quick, Balanced, Deep
Best for: homework, writing, worksheets
SolsticeBETA
For harder questions and long writing. Deep molten gold: the sun at its peak. Solstice 2 is tuned here on Emvryx’s own lessons: 73.6% on HellaSwag (plain base 71.9%), a perfect identity test, 66% on MBPP and 92% on HumanEval (plain base 96%). It runs VryxCode for bigger coding tasks.
Plans: Pro, Mega
Effort: Quick, Balanced, Deep
Best for: long documents, careful answers
ApogeeTRAINED · TESTING
For your toughest challenges. Cold ice blue: the farthest point of an orbit, distant and quiet. Apogee 2 is tuned here on Emvryx’s own lessons, with a perfect identity test. Its 4-bit file is 8.4 GB, so it fits a 12 GB graphics card. It also runs VryxCode.
Plans: Pro, Mega
Effort: Quick, Balanced, Deep
Best for: the hardest work

Effort: how long should it think?

AutoReads your message and picks for you: quick for a greeting or a simple question, deeper for code, maths and hard questions.
QuickAnswers fast and thinks briefly. Best for simple questions. Uses less of your usage.
BalancedA good mix of speed and care. The default.
DeepTakes longer: thinks it through and checks its work. More accurate on hard questions. Uses more of your usage.
