ABOUT EMVRYX

Built from the ground up.

Emvryx’s first models, Ember 2 and Flare 2, were trained from scratch: their own tokenizer, transformer and training run. The 2.x series builds on open Qwen models (Apache 2.0) and is fine-tuned here on Emvryx’s own lessons: looking things up and showing the source, saying “I don’t know”, following your rules. None of ChatGPT’s, Claude’s or Gemini’s answers are in its lessons. Some practice lessons are written by an open model and checked by rules.

Every number here is measured; a dash means not measured yet. HellaSwag is scored on the full 10,042-question test for Ember 2 and Flare 2, and on a 2,000-question sample (about 1 point of wobble) for the models built on Qwen, next to the plain Qwen base they started from: tuning here added 1.6 points on Flare 2.5, 3.7 on Equinox 2 and 1.7 on Solstice 2, and kept the base’s knowledge intact. The identity test checks that each model says it is Emvryx. The 200-question tests have about 3 to 4 points of wobble, so only big gaps mean something: the clear one is maths word problems on Flare 2.5 (double its base). The rules sheet checks Emvryx’s own way of answering, so plain bases score low on it by design. Equinox 2 and Solstice 2 are being run on the public tests, then HumanEval, MBPP and the coding-agent tests; they are added here when measured.

Its own brain

We wrote the tokenizer, the transformer and the training loop, then trained it on carefully chosen reading: educational pages, everyday web text, all of English Wikipedia, code in many languages and step-by-step maths.

Taught like a good assistant

It answers from its notes and names the source. It says “I don’t know” instead of guessing, rechecks when you say it’s wrong, keeps to exact counts and formats, follows a change of topic, and reads through typos.

A family that keeps improving

Every new version keeps everything the last one learned, and must beat it on the same tests before it replaces it. Bigger siblings follow, and the app stays the same.

At home or in the cloud. Private or public.

Any Emvryx model can run wherever you want it, and be shared as widely as you want. Same models, same app; only the address and the guest list change.

What to keep in mind

Like any AI, Emvryx can make mistakes. That is why it shows its sources and which model answered, so you can check what matters, especially for homework, health and money.
