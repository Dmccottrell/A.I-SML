What's new

The short, plain version. Newest first.

October 2026

Your account and sign-in

  • A new sign-in and sign-up screen, with passwords, two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, passkeys (your fingerprint, face or screen lock), and Continue with Google when the owner sets it up.
  • A Settings page with search, and a section just for the owner.
  • Invite codes: type yours in the box on the home page and you land on Create account with it filled in.

Chat

  • A Report button under every answer, so you can say what went wrong.
  • Continue when an answer stops at its length limit, Branch to try another direction, Edit to change what you asked, and Ask a stronger model when an answer isn't good enough.
  • Search your chats, pin them, and put them in folders.
  • Share a chat as a read-only link, or save it as a PDF or Word file.
  • Your day: today's calendar, tasks and mail from your connected apps in one tap.
  • When it can't read a picture, a file or one of your apps, it now says so instead of answering around it.

Connected apps

  • 21 free, read-only apps: calendar, email, news feeds, Todoist, Notion, Slack, Linear, Trello, Jira, Dropbox, GitLab, Hugging Face, Vercel and more. Ask "what is on my calendar tomorrow?" and it looks.

VryxCode, the coding assistant

  • Plan first, a to-do list, Rewind, a live preview beside the chat, a cloud machine you can run code on, and a language chooser that recommends the best language for the job.

Models

  • Flare 2.5, Equinox 2, Solstice 2 and Apogee 2 were retrained with our own lessons. See the scorecard for what each one is good at and where it falls short.
