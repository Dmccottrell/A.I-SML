Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived in a village near the forest.
Whenever she went out, the little girl wore a red riding cloak, so everyone in the village called her Little Red Riding Hood.
One morning, her mother said, "Take this basket of bread to your grandmother. She is sick and it will make her feel better."
The girl walked along the path through the woods. The birds sang and the sun was warm.
Soon she met a wolf. "Where are you going?" asked the wolf.
"To my grandmother's house," said the girl. "She lives on the other side of the forest."
The wolf ran ahead by a shorter road, but the girl stopped to pick flowers for her grandmother.
