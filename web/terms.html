What Emvryx is

Emvryx is an AI assistant: a family of models (Ember, Flare, Equinox, Solstice, Apogee) and a coding assistant, VryxCode. Some models were trained from scratch; others are open models fine-tuned here and credited to their makers in the project's NOTICE file. When VryxCode uses an outside coding model it is labelled as one. {{operator}} runs this copy and may change, pause or stop it.

The AI can be wrong

Answers are generated and can be incorrect, out of date or made up, even when they sound sure. Emvryx tries to show its sources and say "I don't know", but check anything that matters. It is not professional advice: not medical, legal, financial or safety advice. Do not rely on it for emergencies or for decisions where a mistake could hurt someone. Code VryxCode writes can contain bugs or security flaws: read and test it before you use it.
